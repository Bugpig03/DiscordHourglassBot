## ⏳ What is Hourglass?

**Hourglass turns your server's activity into stats people actually want to check.** It tracks time spent in voice and messages sent, then turns them into **leaderboards, XP levels, badges and good-looking stat cards**, right in Discord and on a full web dashboard.

No setup, no config, no prefix: invite it and every slash command works right away.

## 🎴 Stat cards, straight in your channel

Every command answers with an **image card**, drawn in the member's own Discord language and colors.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://hourglassbot.net/static/promo/card-stats.png" alt="/stats card: level, XP, voice time, messages, rank and badges" width="80%">
</p>

| Command | What you get |
|---|---|
| `/stats [user]` | Your card on this server: level, XP, voice time, messages, rank and showcase badges |
| `/allstats [user]` | The same card across every server Hourglass tracks |
| `/top [type] [period] [scope]` | Top 10 by voice time or messages: all time, last 30 days or last 7 days |
| `/alltop` | Top 10 across every server |
| `/versus <player1> <player2> [scope]` | Head-to-head duel between two members, with a winner |
| `/server` | This server's voice time, messages, members and its last 30 days |
| `/help` | Every command |

## 🏆 Levels, badges and streaks

- **XP and levels**: 1 XP per minute in voice, 1 XP per message. Titles from *Curious Novice* to *Hourglass Legend*.
- **9 badges, 7 tiers each** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Legend, Mythic): voice, messages, level, ranking, servers, loyalty, monthly activity, **daily streak** and versatility.
- **Streaks**: keep coming back day after day; the longest run unlocks up to the Mythic tier at 365 days.
- **Rank movements**: see who climbed ▲ or dropped ▼ in the leaderboards.

## 🌐 A full web dashboard: [hourglassbot.net](https://hourglassbot.net)

- **Member profiles** with an activity calendar, records, voice companions, favorite servers and every badge.
- **Customize your profile**: color, banner, background, showcase badges and pinned records. Your bot cards use them too.
- **Server pages** with live voice activity, top members and history.
- **Leaderboards** for members and servers, filtered by period and server, with bots and apps hidden by default.
- **Analytics**: activity curves, peak hours, trends.
- **Versus mode** to compare two members or two servers.

## 🔒 Privacy first

Hourglass counts **how long** members stay in voice and **how many** messages they send. **It never records message content or audio.** Members can read exactly what is stored on the [privacy page](https://hourglassbot.net/privacy).

## 🗣️ 12 languages

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, German, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Hindi, Japanese and Korean. Cards and replies follow each member's Discord language automatically.

## 🚀 Get started in 30 seconds

1. **Invite Hourglass** with the button on this page.
2. Chat and hang out in voice as usual: activity is tracked from the first minute.
3. Type `/stats`, `/top` or `/versus` and share your cards!
