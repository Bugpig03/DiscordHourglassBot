2 Service Description

Hourglass is a free community analytics and gamification platform designed for Discord. Core features include:

  • Tracking cumulative time spent by active members in voice channels.
  • Counting total messages sent in public text channels.
  • Computing experience levels (XP), prestige titles, and milestone badges.
  • Publishing public leaderboards, server activity metrics, and member profiles.
  • Dynamically rendering exportable SVG and PNG statistical cards.
